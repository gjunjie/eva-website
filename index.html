How It Works

Three steps. Three seconds. One smarter decision.

1

Snap

Photograph the nutrition facts panel or ingredient list on any food package.

2

Analyze

AI reads the label with Vision OCR, identifies every ingredient, and translates jargon into plain language.

3

Decide

Get a personalized 0–100 health score, a one-sentence verdict, and Positive / Negative ingredient highlights.

Powerful Features

Everything you need to make informed food choices.

Vision OCR

Apple Vision extracts text from any label — even tiny print, curved surfaces, or cluttered packaging.

AI Ingredient Analysis

Gemini AI identifies each ingredient, classifies its origin, and explains what it actually does in plain language.

Health Scoring

A 0–100 score with clear labels: Great Choice, Good Choice, Not Bad, Think Twice, or Avoid.

Ingredient Cards

Each ingredient gets a detailed card: origin, concern level, and what it means for your health.

Scan History

Every scan is saved locally. Search past results, track your choices, and see your average health score.

Profile Stats

Track total scans, average health score, and build a record of smarter food choices over time.
